    Inspired by our Catholic Faith, Our Lady of the Snows School guides and nurtures students to become young leaders who love God, love learning and love each other.

    Love God

    A personal relationship with God, nurtured by daily prayer, weekly Mass and the sacraments.

    Love Learning

    Challenging coursework that ignites curiosity in the arts and sciences.

    Love Each Other

    Service, compassion and leadership — locally and globally.

    Our mission comes to life through our Schoolwide Learning Expectations: Faith, Knowledge, Leadership and Service.
